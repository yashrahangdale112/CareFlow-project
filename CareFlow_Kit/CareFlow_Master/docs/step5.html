5.4 2-minute demo script (bol-bol ke practice kijiye)

[0:00 – 0:20] Problem
“Hospital me ek patient ko Registration se Discharge tak kai steps se guzarna padta hai. Real me har patient ka rasta alag hota hai. Sirf report dekhne se pata nahi chalta ki kahan wait ho raha hai aur kahan kharcha badh raha hai.”

[0:20 – 0:40] Solution
“Maine CareFlow banaya — ek clinical pathway process mining system. Yeh hospital ke event log ko padhta hai — har row matlab ek patient ka ek step — aur usse asli process model, bottlenecks, conformance aur prediction nikalta hai.”

[0:40 – 1:10] Discovery demo
“Yeh hai hamara discovered process model — Directly-Follows Graph, 28 edges, 15 activities. 1,500 patients ke 13,583 events se bana hai. 14 different pathway variants mile, lekin top 5 variants milkar 81 % cases cover karte hain — matlab zyada tar patients standard pathway follow karte hain.”

[1:10 – 1:40] Insights demo
“Yeh Overview tab. Average stay 9.65 ghante. Sabse bada bottleneck hai Observation — average 226 minute wait. Costliest department Operation Theatre hai. Poore 6 mahine ka treatment cost 2.58 crore rupaye. Load Friday 2 baje peak par hai — matlab staff roster usi hisaab se lagana chahiye.”

[1:40 – 2:00] Prediction demo
“Aur yeh predictive part — main ek model train kiya jo admission ke waqt hi bata deta hai ki patient ka stay 24 ghante se zyada ho sakta hai ya nahi. 4 real-world drivers mile: kitne events hue, rework kitna hua, triage level, aur admission type. Isse hospital pehle se plan kar sakta hai.”

5.5 Viva / review me poochhe jaane wale 12 sawaal

#SawaalAapka jawab (short)
1Process mining kya hai?Event logs se asli business process ko discover, monitor aur improve karne ki technique. 3 hisse: discovery, conformance, enhancement.
2Event log me minimum kya chahiye?Case ID, activity, timestamp — “process mining ke teen zaroori columns”.
3DFG aur Petri net me farak?DFG sirf “kaun kis ke baad aaya” dikhata hai. Petri net me places aur tokens hote hain, isliye conformance properly check ho jaata hai.
4Variant kya hota hai?Ek case ka poora activity sequence. Same sequence wale cases ek hi variant ke hain.
5Conformance checking kaise kiya?Token-based replay — actual trace ko discovered model par chalaya, missing aur remaining tokens gine, usse fitness nikala.
6Fitness 100 % kyun aaya?Kyunki data synthetic hai aur generator ke saare paths model me cover hote hain. Real data par 0.70–0.95 aata hai. (honest answer)
7Data leakage kya hai, aapne kaise handle kiya?Outcome ke baad bante columns (waiting time, service time) model se hata diye — production model leakage-free features par bana hai, tabhi results honest hote hain.
8Imbalanced data kaise handle kiya?Long-stay cases sirf 1.13 % hain, isliye accuracy ki jagah F1, Precision, Recall, ROC-AUC par model select kiya, aur stratified 80/20 split + 5-fold cross-validation use kiya.
9Kaunsa model best nikla?Leakage-free set par Gradient Boosting — accuracy 99.7 %, F1 0.80, ROC-AUC 0.998.
10Business impact kya hai?Bottleneck (Observation wait) ka pata chala, staff roster peak hour ke hisaab se plan ho sakta hai, aur long-stay patients ko pehle se flag karke bed planning ho sakti hai.
11Project ki limitation?Reference data synthetic hai; asli hospital data ke liye ETL aur data-privacy clearance chahiye. Model retraining bhi regular chahiye.
12Aage kya kar sakte hain?Real HIS/EMR integration, live alerting, simulation (what-if), aur object-centric process mining multiple patients ke saath.
